# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to track their daily food intake and nutrition.

## Product Purpose

NutriLog helps users record foods by meal, compare daily calorie and macronutrient intake with personal targets, and review previous days. The MVP succeeds when users can register, save their profile, add food to the diary, and understand their daily progress and history.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- MVP capabilities and exclusions are defined in [docs/MVP-SCOPE.md](docs/MVP-SCOPE.md).
- The web app supports Russian, Ukrainian, and English.
- Food values are stored per 100 g; diary entries preserve a nutrition snapshot for the recorded portion.
- Daily targets use the documented Mifflin–St Jeor, activity, goal, and macro allocation rules.
- User data is personal and must remain isolated by account.
- Mobile applications, barcode scanning, recommendations, and advanced analytics are outside the MVP.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: NutriLog.
- The approved design direction is darker than the current interface, with green shifted toward blue. The user supplied TablicaKalorijnosti.ru for food discovery and two Dribbble sports interfaces as references for contrast and palette.

## Product Principles

- Make daily food logging quick and clear.
- Keep personal targets and diary history easy to understand.
- Show nutrition data with its serving basis and preserve recorded values.
- Keep the MVP focused on reliable tracking workflows.
